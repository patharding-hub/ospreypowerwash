Every package includes the design, installation, maintenance through the season, takedown from January and off-season storage. A 25% materials deposit holds your install date and covers the bulbs and cord cut for your roof; the balance is due once the lights are on.

Two stories, a garage and the beds

The typical EHT front is a two-story roof line, a garage with a gable over it and a planted bed along the front. Classic lights the roof; Classic Plus adds a wreath on the garage gable and the shrubs wrapped to match, which is where most houses here end up.

Neighborhood rules

If you are in a development with an HOA, tell us on the form. Some have rules on colors or on when displays can go up and come down, and we will design to them — the design comes as a picture you can send to the committee first.

No ladder, no attic full of boxes

We bring the lights, put them up, keep them working and take them away in January. You do not need to be home for any of it, and there is nothing to store in the garage for the other eleven months.

What every package includes

A design drawn on your own house

Nothing here is off a shelf. We take a picture of the front of your house and draw your display straight onto it — your colors, your roof line, the trees you actually have — so before you spend anything you are looking at a visual of your own home lit up, not a photo of somebody else’s.

We use our own lights

So you never have to buy them, maintain them, untangle them or find somewhere to keep them. Commercial-grade LED, cut to fit your roof, and ours to look after — no boxes in your garage for eleven months, and no strand that half-works when it comes back out.

Maintained the whole time it is up

We install it and then look after it. A bulb gone, a run gone dark, a strand pulled loose by the wind — one call and we come and fix it, at no charge, for as long as the display is up.

Taken down when you are ready

Takedowns start in January, and you are welcome to keep them lit through to mid-February if you are in no hurry. Whenever you say, the lights come down and go into our storage rather than your attic. Next season you call and it goes straight back up.
