Development vinyl

Houses from the nineties and two-thousands are mostly vinyl, which is the easiest siding there is to bring back — the green comes off completely and the color underneath is usually better than owners expect. Whole streets have the same problem on the same elevation, because they face the same way.

Concrete and pavers

Driveways here are generous, and square footage is what drives the price on concrete. It is also where the size discount tends to kick in, so a drive and a house together often costs less than the two priced separately. Measure both in the estimator and it will show you.
