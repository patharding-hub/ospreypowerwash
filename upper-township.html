Trees, damp and moss

Away from the beach the wind drops and the tree cover goes up, so surfaces stay wet longer. Roofs here collect moss rather than just streaking, and north-facing walls and fences go green quickly. Shaded concrete grows a film that gets slippery, which is worth staying ahead of.

Close to home

Corbin City is inside the township, so this is the shortest run we make. Marmora, Seaville, Tuckahoe, Petersburg and Beesleys Point are all a few minutes away, which makes scheduling easy and makes small jobs worth doing.
