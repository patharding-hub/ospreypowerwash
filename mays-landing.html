What the tree cover does

Tree cover keeps siding, roofs and concrete damp for hours longer after rain, and damp is all the algae needs. It also comes back sooner under a canopy than on an open lot, so a regular wash is worth more here than in most places.

Black streaking on shingles is a common call out here, and it is soft wash work — pressure on a shingle roof buys a clean season at the cost of years of roof life.

The village and the rest of it

The oldest housing sits around the village, and it brings painted wood, porch detail and trim that need the gentle approach. Most of Mays Landing is newer than that: vinyl-sided houses on big wooded lots, where the work is the shaded elevations and a wide concrete driveway.
