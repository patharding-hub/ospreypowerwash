Tree cover means more growth

Anything under a canopy stays damp for hours longer after rain or fog, and that is all algae needs. Houses with trees close by go green faster and more unevenly than exposed ones, and the roof usually shows it before the siding does.

It is also why the north and east elevations here are so often a shade darker than the front of the house. Those walls are where a wash earns its money.

Older houses, painted trim

Older properties bring painted wood trim, porch ceilings and rail that need the gentle treatment. Low pressure and the right solution clean them without lifting sound paint, which a pressure wand cannot promise.
