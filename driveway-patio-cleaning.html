DRIVEWAYS, WALKSAND PATIOS

Concrete is the one place high pressure genuinely belongs, and the one place a careless job shows for years afterwards.

📋 Get Instant Quote 📞 (609) 780-2766

No zebra stripes

Cleaning a drive with a wand alone leaves overlap marks, and on concrete those stripes are permanent until the whole slab is redone. The work is done with a surface cleaner, which keeps the pressure even across the full width and gives you one consistent finish rather than the pattern of how somebody walked.

What comes off

Salt scale from winter, rust marks, tire rubber, the moss that creeps in at the joints and edges, and the general grey that arrives so slowly nobody notices until it is gone. What comes back is concrete that looks new again, and the difference shows from the street.

Pavers take less pressure

Pavers are cleaned with minimal pressure, to preserve the polymeric sand in the joints that holds them in place. Concrete can take the full force of a surface cleaner; pavers are washed at a setting that lifts the moss and the grey without stripping the joints out.

Pricing is by the square foot, with a different rate for pavers than for poured concrete. Measure yours in the estimator for a price on screen.
