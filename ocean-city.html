Salt spray and tight lots

On the properties closest to the beach the salt film never really goes away, and it holds dust the way nothing else does. Lots are narrow, so the shaded side wall of one house often stands three feet from the next. It stays damp all day and greens up first.

That gap is the awkward part of the job. We keep the rinse tight, so we are not cleaning your house onto the neighbor's siding and plants.

Rental turnover

If your place is a summer rental, the outside matters most on the day you take the photographs and on the Saturdays between tenants. You book online, so you pick the date that suits the calendar instead of negotiating it over the phone.

Plenty of Ocean City owners live over in the Philadelphia suburbs and are not at the house midweek. You do not need to be there. Leave the outside tap on and the gate unlocked, and we send photographs when the job is done.
