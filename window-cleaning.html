Glass, frames and screens

A window is not just the pane. Sills and frames hold a surprising amount of dirt, and the screens hold more than either - which is why glass cleaned without doing the screens looks poor again after the first rain.

Screens come out, get washed and go back. It is the step most often skipped and the one that decides how long the job lasts, which is why it is worth adding.

Salt film and hard water

Coastal glass carries a fine salt film that water and a squeegee will simply move around, leaving it streaked. It needs a solution that lifts it.

Hard water spotting is the other one, usually from a sprinkler hitting the glass day after day. Caught early it comes off. Left for years the minerals etch into the surface and no amount of cleaning brings it all back, so it is worth dealing with before it sets.

What it costs

Windows are quoted by the number of panes rather than by area, so tell us how many, and whether you want screen cleaning and frame cleaning added. Add it to a house wash and the frames and screens are cleaned for free.
